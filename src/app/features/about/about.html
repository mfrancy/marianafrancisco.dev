My first experience was on the business side of finance, working closely with products, operations and business rules. Being part of that environment sparked my curiosity about how digital products are built and eventually led me to front-end development.

Today I'm constantly expanding my knowledge beyond the UI. I'm fascinated by application architecture, state management and backend technologies that shape business rules and define products.

Every project is a way to communicate ideas. More than writing code, I enjoy creating experiences that reflect curiosity, attention to detail and the way I see technology.
